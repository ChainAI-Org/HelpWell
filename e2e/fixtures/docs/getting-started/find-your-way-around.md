# Find your way around

Every claim starts on the expenses page. Three things on it do most of the work.

![The expenses page, with New expense, the Status column and Export period marked](../_screenshots/e2e-expenses-page.png)

1. **New expense** files a claim. Drop a receipt on it and the amount fills itself in.
2. **Status** says where each claim is: Draft, Pending, Approved, Exported or Reimbursed.
3. **Export period** closes the month and sends approved expenses to your accounting system.
