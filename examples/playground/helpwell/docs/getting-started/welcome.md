# Welcome to Ridgeline

Ridgeline turns company spending into one reviewed, exported ledger. People
photograph receipts, Ridgeline matches them to card transactions, a manager
approves what needs approving, and finance exports the month to the accounting
system. Nobody assembles a spreadsheet.

## What you set up first

Five things, in this order. Each one works on its own, so you can stop after any
of them and still have something useful.

1. **Your workspace.** Name, base currency, and the start of your fiscal year.
   Settings is at **Settings → Workspace**. Two minutes.
2. **Your team.** Invite people and give them a role. See
   [Invite your team and set roles](/help/getting-started/invite-your-team).
   Five minutes.
3. **A card feed.** Connect the company card so transactions arrive on their own.
   See [Connect a company card](/help/getting-started/connect-a-card).
   Ten minutes, plus a day for the first sync.
4. **An approval policy.** Decide what needs a human and what does not. See
   [How approvals work](/help/approvals/how-approvals-work). Ten minutes.
5. **An accounting export.** Point Ridgeline at Xero, QuickBooks or NetSuite. See
   [Accounting integrations](/help/admin-and-billing/accounting-integrations).
   Fifteen minutes.

## The vocabulary

Ridgeline uses four words with specific meanings, and the rest of the help
center assumes them.

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Expense** | One thing somebody spent money on. Always has an amount, a date, a category and a payer. |
| **Receipt** | The image or PDF that proves an expense. Lives attached to exactly one expense. |
| **Report** | A batch of expenses submitted together for approval. Optional: expenses can be approved one at a time. |
| **Transaction** | A line from the card feed. Matched to an expense, or waiting to be. |

An expense with no receipt is still an expense. A transaction with no expense is
what the **Unmatched** tab is for.

## What Ridgeline does not do

It does not pay anybody. Reimbursements leave through your payroll or your bank,
and Ridgeline marks them reimbursed when the export comes back. It also does not
hold card credentials — the feed is read-only, and read-only is the only access
we ask a bank for.

## Where to go next

If you are the person rolling this out, read
[Approval policies and limits](/help/approvals/approval-policies) before you
invite anybody. Policy is much easier to set before people have habits.

If you were invited by someone else and just want to file something, read
[Submit an expense](/help/expenses/submit-an-expense). That is the whole job.
