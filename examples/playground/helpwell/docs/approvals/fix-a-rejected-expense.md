# Fix a rejected expense

Rejection is not a verdict on you. It is the only way an approver can hand an
expense back so it can be edited, so it is also what they use when a category is
wrong or a receipt is unreadable.

A rejected expense goes back to **Draft** with the reason attached. Nothing is
deleted and you do not refile from scratch.

## Getting it moving again

1. Open it from **My expenses → Rejected**. The reason is at the top.
2. Fix the thing named in the reason.
3. **Submit** again. It goes back to the same approver with the previous reason
   and your change visible in the history, so they are not re-reading it cold.

## The four reasons this usually happens

**The receipt is unreadable.** Re-photograph it flat, in better light, with the
total visible. If the receipt is genuinely gone, use **Declare missing receipt**
— see [Attach and match receipts](/help/expenses/attach-and-match-receipts).

**The category is wrong.** Usually a meal filed as Travel, or a subscription
filed as Supplies. Your workspace's category list is at the top of the picker
with descriptions; if a description is unhelpful, tell whoever has the Finance
role, because the fix is the list rather than the expense.

**It is over a limit and unexplained.** Over-limit expenses are approvable, but
the approver has to justify them to someone else. Add a note saying why before
resubmitting and it usually goes straight through.

**It is a duplicate.** Check **My expenses** for the same amount and date. If it
genuinely is a duplicate, delete this one rather than resubmitting it.

## If you disagree

Reply on the expense. The comment thread is on the record and notifies the
approver, which is better than a message that nobody can find in three months.
If it stays stuck, anybody with the Finance role can reassign the approval at
**Approvals → Reassign**.

## Rejected after a period closed

If the period the expense belongs to has been closed, resubmitting will fail
with "period closed". Ask finance to reopen the period at **Settings → Periods**,
or refile the expense in the current period with a note explaining the original
date. Which of those is right depends on your accounting policy, not on
Ridgeline.
